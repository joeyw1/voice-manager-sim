🗣 Speak more clearly — slow down
Most non-native speakers speak too fast when nervous. Aim for 120–140 words per minute. Pause at commas and full stops. Silence is not awkward — it signals confidence.
🔊 3 most common pronunciation mistakes
1. Final consonants — say the last sound clearly. "Want", not "wan".
2. TH sound — "think" not "tink". Touch tongue to upper teeth.
3. Word stress — "preSENT" (verb) vs "PREsent" (noun). Stress changes meaning.
🎯 Stop talking in circles
Your conclusion must be your FIRST sentence. Say "My recommendation is X" before you explain anything. If your conclusion is not in your first sentence, you are already circling.
📢 5 frameworks — use them every day
PREP — Point, Reason, Example, Point (opinions)
SBA — Situation, Background, Action (updates)
PPP — Problem, Proof, Proposal (presentations)
CLR — Conclusion, Logic, Result (any question)
Buy Time — Repeat, Rephrase, Bridge (when you freeze)
💪 6 power phrases to memorise
"My recommendation is…"
"The bottom line is…"
"To directly answer your question…"
"What I can tell you is…"
"Let me rephrase that to be more precise."
"I'd like to offer a different perspective."
🔑 Replace weak words
"I think maybe" → "I recommend"
"kind of good" → "effective"
"not sure but" → "Based on what I know,"
"we should do something" → "I propose we [action]"
📅 Daily habit (10 minutes)
1 Practice session + 1 Manager Sim every morning before work. 3 weeks of this and your brain restructures how it forms sentences automatically. Consistency beats intensity every time.
